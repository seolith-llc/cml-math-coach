# Puzzle Trail

An invite-only puzzle game for kids in Grades 2 and 3: tough number and thinking puzzles, by voice or touch.
Independent: not affiliated with any competition, testing organization, school or publisher (see Settings → About in the app).

- **Number puzzles:** endless original puzzles across 40 topics, adapting to what your child needs.
- **Test:** 6 to 20 questions. Learn or Test mode, and no clock, a clock for the whole test, or one per question. The default is 6 in 30 minutes.
- **Coach:** reads aloud, listens to answers, gives hints, explains mistakes, and brings missed problems back later.
- **Thinking puzzles:** original reasoning puzzles in three groups: words (analogies, sentence completion, classification), numbers (number analogies, number puzzles, number series) and shapes (figure matrices, paper folding, figure classification). Grade 2 is picture-based and read aloud; Grade 3 uses words. Tap to answer.
- **End-of-round review:** score, feedback per question type, every question's explanation, and "Try missed again" to raise the score.
- **Animated explanations:** every hint and answer is a short narrated picture (number lines, bars, coins, clocks, grids, balances). Play, pause, step back and forward, replay, change speed, mute.
- **Daily goal, stars and a forgiving streak:** small daily wins, visible levels, celebrations, and a clear "done for today".
- **Reminders:** an optional daily reminder on the iPad, a nudge email to the parent after a few days off, the parent dashboard, and a weekly email.
