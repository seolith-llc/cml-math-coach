# For parents

## iPad

1. Open **https://cml-math-coach.seolith.com** in Safari and tap once (Apple needs a tap before the app can speak).
2. Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It then opens full screen and works offline.
3. In **Settings**, choose a voice and turn on **Read aloud**.

Voice answers need Safari itself. Apple doesn't give speech recognition to apps opened from the Home Screen icon, so for hands-free practice use a Safari bookmark.
Also turn on **Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation** on the iPad.
For a nicer device voice, download an *Enhanced* voice in **Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices** (marked ★ in the app).

**Voice commands:** say an answer, or *repeat*, *hint*, *explain*, *skip*, *next*, *slower*, *faster*, *stop*.

**Explanations:** *Hint* plays the picture up to just before the answer. *Answer* (or a second wrong try) plays all of it with the voice-over. Use ◀ ▶ to go step by step, ↺ to watch again, and the speaker button to turn the voice off. The same player is in Learn and in test results (*Explain*).

## Goals, stars and reminders

- **Daily goal:** Settings → Daily goal (5, 10, 15 or 20 problems). Home shows today's progress. When it reaches *Done for today*, that's a good place to stop.
- **Stars:** each topic earns up to three stars as your child moves up a level, and they never lose them. The streak forgives one missed day a week.
- **Daily reminder on the iPad:** add the app to the Home Screen first. Then Settings → Reminder → Daily reminder, and pick a time. It's skipped on days your child already practiced.
- **Reminder emails:** after 3 days without practice (and again at 7), you get one short email. Turn it off on the dashboard (*Practice reminders*) or from the link in the email.

## Parent dashboard

On the iPad: **Settings → Parent dashboard**. Choose a PIN, then **Share dashboard link** to your own phone and bookmark it.
On the dashboard you can add an email for a Sunday-evening summary.

## A good week

| Day | 15–20 minutes |
|---|---|
| Most days | Review, then one round of Practice (10 problems) |
| Twice a week | One Learn topic: read it together, do the example out loud |
| Weekend | One **Test** at 6 questions in 30 minutes (the CML meet). Quiet room, pencil, paper. |

Ask "How do you know?" more than "Is that right?". Praise the strategy, not just the answer.

## Eight weeks to contest day

| Week | Topics | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missing number, comparing results, patterns | Write a number sentence |
| 2 | Coins, smallest amount, price puzzles | Make a table |
| 3 | Calendar, clocks, units | Draw a picture |
| 4 | Ages, giving and moving, multi-step stories | Act it out |
| 5 | Heads and legs, same rate, work backwards | Guess and check |
| 6 | Triangles, rectangles, Venn diagrams, digits | Number the sections |
| 7 | Ordering, matching, brothers and sisters, letter puzzles | Logical reasoning |
| 8 | Timed tests (Total, then Each at 5 min), plus the topics under *Needs help* | All four steps |
