# For parents

## iPad

1. Open your invite email and tap **Accept the invite** (or open **https://puzzletrail.seolith.com** in Safari and sign in with your email address): a sign-in link arrives by email, and a sign-in lasts 30 days from your last visit. Tap once (Apple needs a tap before the app can speak).
2. Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It then opens full screen and works offline. The first time you open it from the icon, sign in once more inside it.
3. In **Settings**, choose a voice and turn on **Read aloud**.

Voice answers need Safari itself. Apple doesn't give speech recognition to apps opened from the Home Screen icon, so for hands-free practice use a Safari bookmark.
Also turn on **Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation** on the iPad.
For a nicer device voice, download an *Enhanced* voice in **Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices** (marked ★ in the app).

**Voice commands:** say an answer, or *repeat*, *hint*, *explain*, *skip*, *next*, *slower*, *faster*, *stop*.

**Explanations:** *Hint* plays the picture up to just before the answer. *Answer* (or a second wrong try) plays all of it with the voice-over. Use ◀ ▶ to go step by step, ↺ to watch again, and the speaker button to turn the voice off. The same player is in Learn and in test results (*Explain*).

## End of a round

After 10 questions (Practice, Thinking Puzzles or Review) your child sees their score, one line of feedback and how each question type went. Tapping any question shows their answer next to the right one and plays its explanation. **Try missed again** gives the missed questions another go, and the score updates (for example *4 → 8 of 10*). Missed questions also come back later in **Review**.

The last round is saved on the iPad: **Last round** on Home (math practice or review) and on the Thinking Puzzles screen reopens it any time, with its explanations and **Try missed again**. With the parent dashboard turned on, the last rounds also show on your dashboard: the score (including fixes), every question, and tap one to see what your child answered and watch the explanation.

## Thinking Puzzles practice

Home → **Thinking Puzzles**. **Practice** mixes all nine question types; tap a type to see its lesson and a worked example first.

- **Words:** analogies, sentence completion, classification.
- **Numbers:** number analogies, number puzzles, number series.
- **Shapes:** figure matrices, paper folding, figure classification.

Grade 2 uses pictures, questions read aloud and 3 choices. Grade 3 uses words and numbers with 5 choices. Every puzzle is original and generated by the app.

Thinking Puzzles answers count toward the daily goal, the streak and the weekly totals, and the weekly email has its own **Thinking Puzzles** section (questions, right first try, the last round's score and each question type) (missed Thinking Puzzles items show up with their type, e.g. *Paper folding*). Tests and the per-topic number lists don't include them.

## Think first

Puzzle Trail is meant to be hard in a good way. **Hint** opens after a real try (one wrong answer, or 30 seconds), and **Answer** only after two tries and every hint. Each day's **Big Puzzle** (Home) is one very hard puzzle with no clock: a good one to work on together. Ask "How do you know?" more than "Is that right?".

## Goals, stars and reminders

- **Daily goal:** Settings → Daily goal (5, 10, 15 or 20 problems). Home shows today's progress. When it reaches *Done for today*, that's a good place to stop.
- **Stars:** each topic earns up to three stars as your child moves up a level, and they never lose them. The streak forgives one missed day a week.
- **Daily reminder on the iPad:** add the app to the Home Screen first. Then Settings → Reminder → Daily reminder, and pick a time. It's skipped on days your child already practiced.
- **Reminder emails:** after 3 days without practice (and again at 7), you get one short email. Turn it off on the dashboard (*Practice reminders*) or from the link in the email.

## Parent dashboard

On the iPad: **Settings → Parent dashboard**. Choose a PIN, then **Share dashboard link** to your own phone and bookmark it.
On the dashboard you can add an email for a Sunday-evening summary.

## A good week

| Day | 15–20 minutes |
|---|---|
| Most days | Review, then one round of Practice (10 problems) |
| Twice a week | One Learn topic: read it together, do the example out loud |
| Weekend | One **Test**: 6 questions in 30 minutes. Quiet room, pencil, paper. |

Ask "How do you know?" more than "Is that right?". Praise the strategy, not just the answer.

## An eight-week trail

| Week | Topics | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missing number, comparing results, patterns | Write a number sentence |
| 2 | Coins, smallest amount, price puzzles | Make a table |
| 3 | Calendar, clocks, units | Draw a picture |
| 4 | Ages, giving and moving, multi-step stories | Act it out |
| 5 | Heads and legs, same rate, work backwards | Guess and check |
| 6 | Triangles, rectangles, Venn diagrams, digits | Number the sections |
| 7 | Ordering, matching, brothers and sisters, letter puzzles | Logical reasoning |
| 8 | Timed tests (Total, then Each at 5 min), plus the topics under *Needs help* | All four steps |
