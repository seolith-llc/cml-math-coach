# Redesign: free, invite-and-approve growth, original brand, tougher thinking

Status: **decided (step077)**. Phase 1 (rebrand, legal pages, 30-day terms on the device) shipped in step077. Phase 2 (accounts, invites, approvals, roles, per-person terms) shipped in step078 behind `AUTH_REQUIRED` (off until the phase 3 cutover). Phase 3 (new address with the app's own sign-in, device hand-off from the old address, invites by email) shipped in step080; the switchover happened in step082: testers invited by email, `MOVE_ON` on, old address hands devices over. Phase 4 (think-first hints and answers, 3 tries for typed answers, ~70% difficulty, magic squares and rule machines, the daily Big Puzzle) shipped in step083; "How did you know?" follow-ups next. Phase 5 after that.

**Decisions (step077):** name **Puzzle Trail**; repository **private** (the owner changes it in GitHub settings); landing page **not indexed**;
child nickname **on the device only**; **invite-only** (no open sign-up and no waitlist); every member **accepts the terms**, access lasts
**30 days**, then they **re-accept** to continue.

## Why

1. **Access does not scale for free.** Cloudflare Access Free has 50 seats. Those seats are shared with every other app in the
   org and cover only people we add by hand. There is no sign-up, no "invite with limited access" and no approval step.
2. **Brand and IP risk.** The app, the repository, the docs and the git history name a real contest and a real test publisher's exam
   (380 mentions in 71 files, plus a public history). Being "prep for X" invites trademark and endorsement questions.
3. **No legal pages.** There is no privacy policy, terms or disclaimer, and the app is for children.
4. **Product direction.** It should be a thinking game that is hard in a good way, not test prep.

## 1. Brand and content (remove all third-party names)

- **New original name**, everywhere: app, title, icons, emails, repository, Pages project and domain.
  Candidates (to be checked on USPTO TESS and app stores before we commit): **Puzzle Trail**, **Think Island**, **Brain Stones**.
- **Tracks:**
  - The math track becomes **Number Puzzles**.
  - The reasoning track becomes **Thinking Puzzles** (analogies, series, matrices, odd one out, folding: generic puzzle genres).
  - No exam names, no "Level 8/9" or battery language. The grade still decides 3 picture choices (Grade 2) or 5 word choices (Grade 3).
- **Other renames:**
  - "Meets" become **Challenges**.
  - The weekly email, the parent dashboard, Learn, the tester guide and all code identifiers (old track and topic ids) are renamed, with a storage migration so no progress is lost.
- **Audit pass on every generator:** problems stay original and generated per seed. Nothing is copied or paraphrased from any contest paper, book or video.
- **The old names in git history:** the repository is renamed, and either **made private** (recommended, free) or its history is rewritten.
  Rewriting does not remove forks or caches that already exist.

## 2. Legal pages (drafts by me, review by a lawyer recommended)

- **Disclaimer** (on the landing page, in Settings → About, and in the email footer). Suggested wording:
  > *Name* is an independent, free puzzle game for kids. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sanctioned by any math
  > competition, testing organization, school, or publisher, and it does not prepare for any specific exam. All puzzles are
  > original and generated by the app, inspired by the kinds of puzzles families enjoy and share online. No guarantee of results.

  "Inspired by challenges seen on YouTube" is softened on purpose: it should not suggest that anything was copied from a channel.
- **Privacy policy:**
  - **Who has an account:** parents only.
  - **What is collected:**
    - **Parent:** email.
    - **Child (no account):** nickname (optional), grade, answers and progress.
    - **Device:** a push subscription, if turned on.
    - **Feedback:** what someone sends with the Feedback button.
  - **Why:** to run the app. No ads, no selling, no third-party analytics or trackers, and a session cookie only.
  - **Where it is stored:** Cloudflare (data and hosting) and Amazon SES (email).
  - **Retention:** idle families are deleted after 18 months; logs after 90 days.
  - **Rights and contact:** export and delete the whole family in one tap, and an email address for questions.
  - **Children:** a COPPA section on parental consent. A parent signs up with a verified email and consents for their children. Data is used for internal operations only.
- **Terms of use:** a free service, as is, invite-only, with rules on misuse, suspension and changes.
- **Security and retention program:** a short written policy. The 2025 COPPA amendments expect one from child-directed services.

## 3. Accounts, invites and approvals (replaces Cloudflare Access for the app)

No passwords. Sign-in uses a magic link sent by email through SES (we already use it). Sessions live in D1 behind an HttpOnly cookie.
Sign-up is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile (free) and rate limits.

| Role | Who | Can |
|---|---|---|
| **Super admin** | you | approve or deny the waitlist, suspend a family or an invite branch, see counts (no answers). The admin page stays behind Cloudflare Access (1 seat). |
| **Family owner** | the parent who signed up | everything for the family: children, settings, the dashboard, invites, export and delete |
| **Co-parent** | invited by the owner | the same, except deleting the family or removing the owner |
| **Viewer** (limited) | grandparent, tutor, teacher | read-only progress dashboard; no settings, invites or feedback |
| **Child profile** | no email, no login | plays on a device a parent signed in; the parent area stays behind the PIN |

**How people get in (invite-only, organic growth, gated):**
1. **Join my family** (co-parent or viewer): the owner creates the invite, so the invitee is in as soon as they confirm their email and accept the terms.
2. **Invite a friend's family:** an approved parent sends an invite link. When the friend signs up, **the inviter approves with one tap** in an email.
   If the inviter does not answer in 72 hours, it goes to the super admin.
3. **Super admin invites** seed new families directly (approved on accept).
   There is no open sign-up and no waitlist: without a valid invite link, the sign-up page only says it is invite-only.
4. **Terms, 30 days at a time:** at first sign-in and again every 30 days, each member sees the terms, privacy policy and disclaimer and must accept them.
   Until they re-accept, the app shows only that screen. The child's device keeps playing offline, and syncing resumes after the parent re-accepts.
   Each acceptance records the terms version and time. A new terms version asks again right away.
5. **Invite quota:** 3 to start, plus 1 per week of active practice, up to 10. Every account records who invited it, so a bad branch can be paused at once.
6. **Capacity guard:** sign-ups pause by themselves near free-tier limits (Workers requests, D1 writes, TTS neurons), and the app falls back to the device voice.

**Public pages** (the only ones, all `noindex`): landing, privacy, terms, sign-in, invite accept, and the existing token-protected email links.
Everything else requires a session. Bots still get a 403 (`server/guard.ts`).

**Cost stays $0** on the Cloudflare free tier (Pages, Functions, D1, Turnstile, Workers AI daily allowance) plus SES at about $0.10 per 1,000 emails.
If the repository goes private, the hourly GitHub cron moves to a free Cloudflare Worker cron to save Actions minutes.

**Migration:** current testers and families are created as approved owners and their progress is kept.
Cloudflare Access comes off the app hosts only after the new sign-in is live and tested.

## 4. Tougher thinking (make kids think)

- **Think before help:**
  - The first hint unlocks after a real try (one wrong answer or 30 seconds).
  - The answer is shown only after 2 tries and all hints.
  - The explanation always follows.
- **Typed answers for number puzzles** (no guessing from choices). Choices stay only where the puzzle is visual.
- **Desirable difficulty:** the adaptive level aims for about 70% first-try success, not 90%. Stars need wins at the harder levels.
- **New original puzzle families:** balance scales, work backwards, cage sums, magic squares, mini logic grids,
  fewest-moves puzzles, coin weighing, and "find the rule, then use it" patterns.
- **A daily "Big Puzzle":** one very hard puzzle with no clock, meant to be worked on with a parent and celebrated however long it takes.
- **Explain it back:** after a right answer, now and then, "How did you know?" offers 3 reasons, and the right reason earns a "Thinker" sticker.
- Timers become opt-in (Challenges only). No loss-framed copy, and the daily goal still ends play.
