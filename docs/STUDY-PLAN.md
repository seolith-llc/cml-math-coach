# Coaching guide & 8-week study plan

## How the coach helps a child learn
1. **Read → think → answer.** The coach reads the problem aloud (a 7–8-year-old's reading speed shouldn't limit the maths).
2. **First mistake → targeted nudge.** If the wrong answer matches a known misconception, the child hears *why*
   (for example "That is one of the answers, now find how much more"). Otherwise they get the next hint.
3. **Second mistake → worked solution.** The steps are read one at a time and highlighted, and the problem goes into the review queue.
4. **Spaced review.** Missed problems come back the same day, then after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days, until the child gets them right repeatedly.

## Daily routine (15–20 minutes)
| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 3 | Review mistakes (🔁) |
| 10 | Smart practice: one 10-problem session |
| 3–5 | Learn one topic page and say the strategy out loud |

Once a week, do a **Mock meet** under real conditions: quiet room, pencil and scrap paper, 30-minute timer.

## 8-week plan
| Week | Focus topics | Strategy of the week |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missing number, compare results, patterns | Write a number sentence |
| 2 | Money (coins, smallest amount, price puzzles) | Make a table |
| 3 | Time & calendar, units | Draw a picture / look for a pattern |
| 4 | Age, giving & moving, multi-step stories | Act it out |
| 5 | Heads & legs, same rate, work backwards | Guess and check |
| 6 | Counting triangles/rectangles, Venn, digit counting | Number the sections |
| 7 | Logic ordering & matching, letter-digit puzzles | Use logical reasoning |
| 8 | Mixed mock meets (2–3) and review the weakest topics on the Progress page | Pólya's 4 steps |

## Tips for parents and coaches
- Ask "How do you know?" rather than "Is that right?". Explaining out loud builds reasoning.
- Praise strategies and effort ("You drew a picture, nice!"), not just correct answers.
- Let the child use scrap paper. Real meets are pencil-and-paper.
- If a topic stays below 50% mastery, go back to its Learn page and do the worked example together.
