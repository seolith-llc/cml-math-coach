One problem card

The coach allows two tries. A wrong answer gets a targeted message and a hint; a second miss shows the answer and the animated explanation. Pick a scenario.

    The sum of two numbers is 23. One number is 7 more than the other. What is the larger number?

    Bold key words get the Neon highlighter. The voice never reads the markers.

    Spaced review (Leitner boxes)

    A missed problem starts in box 1. Each right answer moves it up and pushes the next review further out; a wrong answer sends it back to box 1. Right in box 5: mastered.

    Mastery, level and stars

    After every answer: mastery ← 0.75 × mastery + 0.25 × (right ? 1 : 0). The level picks the next problem's difficulty; stars only ever go up.

    The parent's email

    With a confirmed email: a weekly digest on Sunday from 5 pm Central, and practice reminders after a break: the first after 3 idle days, the second after 7, at 5 pm Central, never on Sunday. Practising again resets the count.
