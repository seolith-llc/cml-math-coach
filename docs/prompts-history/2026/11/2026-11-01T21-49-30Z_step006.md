## Response summary

**Approach:** neural text-to-speech from **Cloudflare Workers AI (Deepgram Aura-2, `@cf/deepgram/aura-2-en`)**, served by a Pages Function on the same Cloudflare project. There are no third-party keys, and the device voice remains the automatic offline fallback. The model id, the 40 speaker names and the input schema were checked against `@cloudflare/workers-types` (the docs site is blocked from this session).

**Server**
- `functions/api/tts.ts`: `GET /api/tts?v=<voice>&t=<text>` returns MP3.
  - Voice allow-list, 600-character cap, same-origin check.
  - Handles every output shape Workers AI may return.
  - Edge cache (Cache API, kept for one year, files never change).
- `wrangler.toml`: `pages_build_output_dir = "dist"`, `[ai] binding = "AI"`.

**Client** (`src/voice/speech.ts`)
- Engine `premium | device`. Premium is the default and splits text into chunks of about 280 characters at sentence ends.
- The next 2 chunks are fetched ahead, played through one shared audio element, and talking speed uses playbackRate.
- Clips are cached in memory, and the service worker caches them too (CacheFirst `coach-voice`, 400 entries, 60 days), so they replay offline.
- If the service fails, the app switches to the device voice and backs off for 60 seconds.
- iOS: the first tap also unlocks HTMLAudio (silent WAV).
- Better device-voice choice: Premium, Enhanced, Natural and Neural voices are ranked first and marked ★; novelty voices are ranked last.

**Settings UI:** a "Coach voice" choice (✨ Premium natural / 📱 Device), a 12-voice premium picker with ▶ Preview, a device-voice picker with ★ and an iPad tip about downloading Enhanced/Premium Siri voices. The privacy note was updated.

**Verification**
- 261 tests pass. New function tests cover the happy path, 400/413/403/502 responses and output normalisation. The build passes.
- Playwright with a mocked `/api/tts`: a lesson is read as 2 premium chunks (276 characters max). On a 502 it falls back to the device voice. No page errors.
