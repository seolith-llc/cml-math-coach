## Request (verbatim)

> We want to setup notifications and nudges for users to practice and complete tasks and move to the next level. We want it to be made sticky and retentive. Apply all the best practices

## Response summary

**Principles.** This is a children's app, so retention comes from mastery, small goals and parent-controlled reminders. Following the UK Age-Appropriate Design Code and COPPA guidance, there is no streak pressure, no loss-framed copy, and no notifications aimed at the child that the parent didn't turn on.

**Levels you can see (`core/progress.ts`)**
- Each topic earns ★/★★/★★★ when the adaptive level rises (thresholds = `mastery.levelFor`), and stars never go down.
- `recordAttempt` returns events. Practice, review and learn-mode tests celebrate with a toast (queued, never overlapping) and a spoken line if Read aloud is on.
- Stars show on Learn, the Home Learn row and Progress (Day streak · Stars · First try).

**Daily goal and Today**
- Settings → Daily goal 5/10/15/20 (default 10).
- The Home Today card shows "4 of 10" plus a progress bar and **Practice**. Once met it shows "Done for today", and Keep going becomes secondary (also at the end of a round).
- The Home list only shows Review when something is due. Test shows ✓ once a test was done this week.

**Streak**
- Local calendar days, fixing a bug where `today()` used the UTC date and moved evening practice to the next day.
- One missed day per 7 is forgiven.

**Parent nudges (`server/email.ts` sendNudges)**
- After 3 days without practice, then 7; at most 2 per break, reset by new practice. 5 pm Chicago, never Sunday, at most once a day.
- Positive subject: "Sam: a quick math round today?".
- Dashboard switches for **Weekly summary** and **Practice reminders**, and a per-kind one-click unsubscribe (`&k=nudges` / `&k=weekly`).

**Device reminders (web push, `server/push.ts`, `src/notify/reminders.ts`, `public/push-sw.js`)**
- RFC 8291 aes128gcm encryption and RFC 8292 VAPID ES256 in WebCrypto, with no dependencies. The VAPID keys are generated once into D1 `kv`.
- Opt-in from Settings → Reminder (permission asked on tap). One a day at the chosen local hour (7 am–8 pm); skipped if the app reported practice today; paused after 14 idle days.
- TTL 4 h, topic replaces stale reminders; expired subscriptions (404/410) are removed; only real push-service endpoints are accepted.
- On iPad it needs the Home Screen app: the switch is disabled with "Home Screen app" in a Safari tab.
- The app icon badge shows reviews due (Badging API).

**One scheduler**
- `.github/workflows/hourly.yml` → `POST /api/hourly` (bearer) runs reminders, nudges and the weekly summary; each decides if it's due.
- It replaces `weekly-email.yml`, and keeps a *force_weekly* manual input.

**Data**
- `migrations/0004_engagement.sql`: family prefs and nudge state, `push_subs`, `kv`.
- Settings gained `goal` and `reminder`, migrated and tested.
- Status reports reminder subscriptions and nudge state.
