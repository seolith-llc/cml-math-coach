## Response summary

**Design.** An explanation is pure data: a `Scene` is a list of steps, and each step holds a voice-over line (`say`) and the full picture at that moment. The player compares one picture with the next by shape id: shapes that are still there move or change colour, new ones appear (lines draw themselves), and missing ones fade out. Generators stay pure and testable, and the renderer is about 150 lines.

**Engine**
- `src/core/scene.ts`: shape types, `SceneBuilder` (add/set/setAll/remove with `prefix*`/step/build) and helpers (text, rect, circle, line, path, arc, brace). Pulses fire once.
- `src/core/explain.ts`: `sceneFor(problem)` returns the problem's scene, or a whiteboard of its written steps as a fallback.
- `src/ui/player.ts`: an SVG stage with CSS-transform animation. Controls are replay, back, play/pause, next, a "k / n" counter, speed (1×, 1.5×, 0.75×) and voice on/off, plus space and arrow keys. The voice uses the chosen natural or device voice. Reduced motion is respected.
- A step marked `reveal` is the first one that gives the answer away. **Hint** plays up to just before it, one more step per tap. **Answer** (or a second wrong try) plays everything.

**Illustrations for all 40 topics** (`src/content/scenes/`)
- `kit.ts`: number lines, hops, tape bars, coins, clock face with hands that always turn forward, people, spread.
- `number.ts`: missing numbers, balance equations, compare, sum and difference, distance, rounding, temperature, patterns, riddles, digits, factors.
- `word.ts`: ages, transfers, three bars, money bars, heads and legs, unit rate, working backwards, shopping.
- `money-time.ts`: coins, calendars, elapsed time, watches, clock hands, lengths and segments.
- `logic-puzzles.ts`:
  - Ordering: slots fill in as clues remove possible orders, with a counter of orders left.
  - Matching grid: ✗ and ✓ marks.
  - Brothers and sisters: who counts, plus a table of tries.
  - Counting: triangles by section, rectangles by size, Venn regions lighting up, handshakes drawn edge by edge.
  - Puzzles: cryptarithm columns, ★ recipes, shape equations, packages checked row by row, and a balance where things are swapped and removed.
  - Palindromes: mirror arcs, spot-the-palindrome, next palindrome.
  - Boards: magic boards (shared circles first) and the corner square.
- Used in Practice and Review (problem card), Learn (topic example), and test results (**Explain**).
