## Request (verbatim)

> tune the voice pauses between steps

## Response summary

**Problem.** The voice started the moment a frame changed, while shapes were still moving. It left only 350 ms after each line. Silent mode advanced at about 180 words a minute, which is too fast for Grade 2–3 readers.

**Change.** The timing rules live in the new `src/core/pacing.ts`, a pure module with tests. Every value is divided by the playback speed.
- **Lead-in:** 450 ms before speaking (250 ms on the first frame), so the picture moves first and eyes and ears don't compete.
- **Think time after each line:** 600 ms, plus 20 ms per word up to +400 ms. It is 1.1 s after the step that reveals the answer, and 1.4 s on the last step.
- **Silent mode:** about 120 words a minute (500 ms per word, at least 2.2 s), with +0.8 s on the reveal step.
- **Safety net:** if the voice "finishes" too soon (no audio, autoplay blocked), the step still stays up long enough to read.
