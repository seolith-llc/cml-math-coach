## Request (verbatim)

> Can we also have one tab for COGAT practices?

## Response summary

**Scope.** A separate CogAT track covering all three batteries and nine question types, with original items in the CogAT style.
- Grade 2 follows Level 8: picture-based, read aloud, 3 choices.
- Grade 3 follows Level 9: words and numbers, 5 choices. Most Level 9 items have five choices; I couldn't confirm Level 8's choice count from public sources.
- Answers are tap-to-pick.

**Architecture**
- `Topic.track` is `'cml'` (the default) or `'cogat'`.
- `topicsForGrade(grade, track = 'cml')` keeps the CML mix, tests (`makeMeet`), Learn, Progress and stars CML-only without touching those screens.
- **New answer UI:** `ui/choicePicker.ts`. Any `choice` answer can carry `pictures` (SVG or emoji).
  - In Practice a tap checks right away; in a Test it selects.
  - Voice works by letter or by saying the option's name. The option name now wins over a same-sounding letter ("bee" vs B). "(c) …" also parses.
- **`content/cogat/`**
  - `figures.ts`: one geometry for 9 shapes × fill, size, count and direction. It outputs SVG markup (questions, tap targets) and scene shapes (animations). It also covers dots, abacus rods and paper folding (the folded outline, and holes mirrored as each fold opens).
  - `common.ts`: `mc()` builds de-duplicated choices, with misconceptions tied to specific wrong options.
  - **Verbal:** analogies (picture relations for G2, plus word relations for G3: baby animals, opposites, tools, workplaces), sentence completion (22 spoken picture questions, 22 written sentences), classification (tagged items; a wrong option never shares any group all three stimuli share).
  - **Quantitative:** number analogies (the rule must be unique across both pairs; dots for G2), number puzzles (bar model; dots for G2), number series (+k, −k, alternating, growing, doubling, repeating; abacus rods for G2).
  - **Nonverbal:** figure matrices (one change for G2, two for G3), paper folding (one fold for G2; two folds or a diagonal for G3), figure classification.
- Every question type has an animated, narrated explanation; hints stop before the reveal.
- **Screen:** `#/cogat` has a Practice button (the mixed round, `#/practice/cogat`) and lists grouped by battery with stars. Each row opens its lesson and example. The Home list gains a **CogAT** row.

**Quality fixes from visual review**
- A box with 2 or 3 copies always looks small, so size now counts only for single shapes (normalized). "Looks small" can no longer be an accidental shared trait.
- Duplicate stimuli are rejected.
- Trivial puzzles such as "1 + ? = 1 + 17" are rejected.
- The abacus and the paper-folding strip were enlarged.
