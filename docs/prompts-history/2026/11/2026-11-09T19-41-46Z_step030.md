## Request (verbatim)

> fix the "✓ with help" label

## Response summary

**Problem.** The dashboard's Mistakes list showed "✓ with help" whenever no wrong answer was typed. That lumped three different things together: the child tapped Answer to reveal it, got it right after a hint, or skipped. Choice answers showed a bare letter. The weekly email showed nothing at all for a revealed answer.

**Fix.** A new `insights.outcomeLabel()` is shared by the dashboard (`screens/parent.ts`) and the weekly email (`server/digest.ts`, HTML and text):
- **Showed the answer:** revealed without answering.
- **Skipped**
- **Right after a hint**
- **45¢:** the first wrong answer.
- **45¢, then right**
- **(b) octopus:** choice answers show the option, not the letter.

The row label is now "Yours", matching the end-of-round compare. The email reads "Yours: … · Correct: …".

**Verification**
- `npm run check`: 729 tests. New label tests cover every case and the choice letter; the weekly email test was updated.
- Two-browser end-to-end run: a revealed CogAT answer shows "Showed the answer" on the dashboard; no page errors.
