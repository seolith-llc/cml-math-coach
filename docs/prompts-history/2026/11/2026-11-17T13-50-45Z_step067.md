## Response summary

**Rules:** `content/stickers.ts` (pure, tested).
- Stickers are earned only at round ends, never per answer, for Fixed a miss (first), New star, Today's goal, and Careful round (≥ 80 % first try, 5+ questions).
- At most 3 a day. Labels name effort, never ability.
- 24 original die-cut SVG designs (8 shapes × 3 colourways) in a fixed surprise order: new designs until all are collected, then they repeat.

**Storage:** `state.stickers` holds {design, kind, at, grade}. `awardStickers` triggers a sync; `stickersToday` applies the cap. The stickers sync as `snapshot.stickers` (last 100), with no schema change.

**Round end:** `roundEnd({ celebrate: roundStart(state) })`, Practice and Review only.
- A celebration card above the score: the buddy cheers about effort ("You fixed every miss!", "What a careful round!", "You went back and fixed it!", "You kept going. That's how we grow!").
- Confetti sized to the score (8 + 2 per right or fixed answer, up to 36), and a chime.
- New stickers pop in with their reason.
- A fix in Try missed again earns "Fixed a miss" then.
- Reopening a saved round (`#/round`) awards nothing.

**Screens**
- `#/stickers` sticker book (a world-map row with the count): only what was collected, newest first, why and when. No empty slots, no "x of 24".
- Parent dashboard: a Stickers section with the latest 12 and their reasons (`insights.recentStickers`, tolerant of bad data).

**Tests:** `tests/stickers.test.ts` (rules, cap, priority, labels, 24 distinct designs and order, storage/day count, dashboard filter). `npm run check` passes with 831 tests.
